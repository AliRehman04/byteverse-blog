This guide compares those tools by what they do for a YouTube channel, what their free plans include and where each free plan runs out. Every price and limit below was checked on the tool's official pricing page or in YouTube Help on November 4, 2026. ## Choose Between Video Generation and Video Editing

Start with the output you need, not the number of AI features advertised:

- **New footage from a prompt or reference image:** use a generator such as Runway. A five-second generated shot is not a finished five-minute YouTube video; narration, sequencing and review still need work.
- **A better cut of footage you already recorded:** start with CapCut for timeline editing or Descript for transcript editing. Buying generation credits does not fix a confusing explanation or an overlong introduction.
- **Vertical clips from an existing recording:** use OpusClip for candidate moments, then check whether each clip makes sense without the full conversation.
- **A thumbnail or a voiceover:** Canva and ElevenLabs address those assets, not the whole edit. Keep their budget separate from generated video credits.

Script-to-video assembly is another workflow: arranging stock or generated scenes around narration. Do not equate that with generating every frame from scratch, or expect a thumbnail tool to replace a video editor.

## Best Free AI Tools for YouTube Creators: Start Inside YouTube Studio

The cheapest AI tools for a channel are the ones YouTube already includes. Three are worth using before you subscribe to anything else.

### Ask Studio for Ideas and Script Feedback

Ask Studio is the AI assistant built into YouTube Studio. YouTube describes it as a creative partner for brainstorming video ideas, getting channel insights and analyzing performance. Two features matter most for video creation. You can paste a script and get feedback based on creative best practices, and you can select a draft, private or unlisted video to get suggestions based on those best practices and your channel's historical retention data before you publish. It can also suggest custom thumbnails after analyzing a video's transcript and themes.

Ask Studio is available to most creators, but only in a web browser on a computer. Made for Kids channels, Official Artist Channels and creators under 18 do not have access. YouTube deletes channel-connected conversations after 30 days and says human reviewers may read them, so do not paste anything confidential into the chat.

### A/B Test Titles and Thumbnails Without an Extension

YouTube Studio can test up to three titles, three thumbnails or title and thumbnail combinations on one long-form video, then show every viewer the version that earned the most watch time. YouTube says a test should finish within two weeks. You need a computer and advanced features enabled on your channel. Shorts, scheduled live streams and premieres cannot be tested, and neither can videos that are private, made for kids or marked for mature audiences.

Export every test thumbnail at 1280 x 720 or larger. If any thumbnail in an experiment is below 720p, YouTube downscales all of the experiment's thumbnails to 854 x 480.

### YouTube's Own Generative Tools for Shorts

The Shorts camera includes experimental AI features that generate images or video clips from a prompt, either as a green screen background or as a standalone clip in your Short. YouTube says they are available in most countries when your YouTube device language is set to English, and Shorts made with these tools are labeled as AI content automatically.

The separate YouTube Create app goes further. Its Gemini Omni feature generates vertical or horizontal clips up to 10 seconds long, and Edit with AI turns 1 to 25 of your own clips into a first draft with music, voiceover and captions. Both are experimental and limited to a list of countries that includes the United States, India and Indonesia; Pakistan was not on either list when we checked.

## Best AI Video Generator for YouTube B-Roll: Runway

Runway generates video from text prompts and reference images. On a YouTube channel, its useful job is filling gaps: an establishing shot you could not film, a visual metaphor for an abstract idea or a short transition between talking-head segments. Runway charges credits by clip length, so plan generated clips as inserts, not as a whole video.

The credit math decides whether Runway fits your budget. The Free plan gives 125 credits once, not every month, along with 5 GB of storage and a selection of models for testing ideas. The Standard plan costs USD 15 a month billed monthly, or USD 144 billed yearly (USD 12/month equivalent), and includes 625 credits a month, which Runway equates to about 52 seconds of Gen-4.5 video, 104 seconds of Gen-4 Turbo or 78 Gen-4 images at 1080p. Runway lists Gen-4.5 at 60 credits per five seconds, so Standard buys ten complete five-second Gen-4.5 generations with 25 credits left. That is a generation budget, not ten usable shots: discarded takes and retries can consume it. Standard also lists no watermarks and 4K upscaling.

Two habits prevent problems later. If a generated clip shows a realistic scene that did not happen, answer the AI use question when you upload (see the rules section below). And keep a note of the prompt, model and plan behind each clip, because commercial terms differ between plans and can change. ## Best AI Video Editors for YouTube: CapCut vs Descript

If you film yourself, your screen or real places, the editor matters more than the generator. CapCut and Descript approach editing from opposite directions.

### CapCut: Free Timeline Editing and Auto Captions

CapCut is a timeline editor with desktop, web and mobile versions, templates, effects and AI features. Its Free plan includes free templates and basic editing tools. CapCut's own caption page says the AI caption feature in CapCut Web is free to use, supports more than 20 languages and accepts an SRT file if you already have captions. Burned-in captions help viewers who watch without sound, which matters most on Shorts.

CapCut Pro adds 1,200 AI credits a month, all premium templates and features, and 1 TB of storage. CapCut displayed Pro at PKR 2,900 a month billed monthly for our location and notes that plan availability and features vary by region, platform and current offer. Check the exact feature inside the version you use (desktop, web or mobile) before building a weekly workflow around it. New accounts were also offered 100 welcome credits to try Seedance video generation; that is a sign-up offer, not a recurring free allowance. Our [step-by-step AI video editing guide](/blog/how-to-edit-videos-with-ai-2026) walks through a free editing workflow in more detail.

### Descript: Edit the Transcript to Edit the Video

Descript transcribes your recording and lets you edit the video by editing the text. Delete a sentence from the transcript and the matching footage disappears. This approach suits talking-head videos, tutorials and interviews, where many edits remove mistakes, pauses and repeated takes. Descript transcribes 25 languages.

The Free plan includes 60 media minutes a month, 100 AI credits that do not renew, limited use of the Underlord AI co-editor and 720p local export, and Descript says it does not require a credit card. Media minutes count everything you import or record, so two 30-minute raw recordings use the whole month. Hobbyist costs USD 192 per person billed yearly (USD 16/month equivalent), or USD 24 per person billed monthly. It raises the limits to 10 media hours and 400 AI credits a month, with watermark-free 1080p export and AI tools such as Studio Sound, Remove Filler Words and Create Clips. Creator costs USD 288 per person billed yearly (USD 24/month equivalent), or USD 35 per person billed monthly, and adds 30 media hours, 800 AI credits a month and 4K export.

## Best AI Tool for Turning Long Videos Into YouTube Shorts: OpusClip

OpusClip takes a long video, picks moments it expects to work as short clips, adds animated captions and reframes them to vertical 9:16. On the free plan, you can upload a local file of up to 10 GB or import from YouTube.

The free plan is useful for evaluation if its watermark and retention limits fit your needs; a watermark alone does not prohibit publishing. OpusClip's plan table lists animated captions with a watermark on Free, no watermark-free export, MP4 exports limited to three days at up to 1080p, media storage that expires after three days, 9:16 reframing only and no virality score. Starter, listed at USD 15 a month on the monthly plan, removes the watermark, adds the virality score, keeps exports for 30 days and adds imports from sources such as Google Drive, Vimeo and Zoom. Pro, at USD 29 a month on the monthly plan, adds 100 GB of storage, 1:1 and 16:9 reframing, and export to Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve. The expiry concerns access to hosted exports, not an MP4 you already downloaded.

Automatic clipping is a starting point, not an editor. Watch every clip before posting, because an automatic cut can stop before the payoff of a sentence or lose the context that made the moment interesting. A practical free workflow is to use OpusClip's picks as a shortlist, then cut the final Shorts from your original footage in CapCut. Cutting from your own long videos also avoids the main reused content problem, which is borrowing someone else's material without changing it significantly.

ElevenLabs turns a script into narration and offers voice cloning on paid plans. It suits faceless channels and narration over B-roll, where nobody needs to appear on camera.

## A Free Weekly YouTube Workflow and Its Limits

Start with your own footage and narration to keep paid generation out of the workflow. This is a suggested process, not a measured weekly output promise; confirm the features and remaining allowance in your account before relying on them:

1. Plan the topic and outline with Ask Studio, then draft the script with your usual AI chatbot and paste it back into Ask Studio for feedback.
2. Record, then edit in CapCut, or in Descript if your raw footage stays under 60 minutes a month.
3. Add auto captions in CapCut Web.
4. Design two or three thumbnails in Canva Free and run a YouTube A/B test on the upload.
5. Run the finished video through OpusClip Free to shortlist Short ideas, then cut the final Shorts from your original footage.
6. Answer YouTube's AI use question honestly if the video includes realistic generated footage.

The free plans do not run out at the same time. This is where each one stops:

| Free plan | Published limit | When it stops a weekly channel |
|---|---|---|
| Descript Free | 60 media minutes a month | After about two 30-minute raw recordings |
| ElevenLabs Free | 10,000 credits a month, no commercial license | Immediately, if the channel is monetized |
| Runway Free | 125 credits, once | After a few test generations |
| OpusClip Free | Watermarked clips, three-day exports | When you need watermark-free output or longer access to hosted exports |
| Canva Free | Shared monthly AI allowance for metered features | When the selected feature reaches your account's allowance |

When one limit blocks your weekly upload, pay for that tool only. Upgrading one bottleneck at a time keeps your costs tied to what the channel actually uses.

### Free AI Video Creation Without a Watermark

A free editor, free generation credits and a free watermark-free export are three different offers. OpusClip's Free export is watermarked, while Runway lists watermark removal on Standard. Neither should be presented as an unlimited, watermark-free generator.

Before editing a full video, make a short test project with the exact captions, templates, music and AI features you intend to use. Check the export dialog for paid assets, resolution restrictions and branding; then watch the downloaded file. A free basic edit does not establish that every AI effect or template exports free. If an asset requires payment, replace it with your own or properly licensed free material instead of assuming a trial covers ongoing publishing.

## YouTube Rules for AI Videos You Must Follow

AI tools make videos faster. They do not change YouTube's rules, and two of those rules matter for every channel that uses them.

### Disclose Realistic AI Content

Not every AI-assisted video needs disclosure. YouTube requires creators to disclose AI-generated or meaningfully altered content that seems realistic. When you upload in YouTube Studio, go to the Attributes section and answer the "AI use" question. Disclosure is required when the content:

- makes a real person appear to say or do something they did not do;
- alters footage of a real event or place;
- generates a realistic scene that did not actually occur;
- creates music that is the main focus of the video.

Non-realistic content and minor edits do not need disclosure, and videos made with YouTube's own generative tools are labeled automatically. YouTube says disclosure alone does not limit a video's audience or its eligibility to earn money. Creators who consistently fail to disclose can face labels added by YouTube, removal of content or suspension from the YouTube Partner Program.

### Avoid Mass-Produced, Repetitive Videos

To earn money on YouTube, a channel is expected to publish original, authentic content that is not mass-produced, generic, repetitive or manipulative. On July 15, 2025, YouTube renamed its "repetitious content" policy to "inauthentic content" to make it clearer that mass-produced and repetitive videos are not eligible for monetization. AI use alone is not a monetization violation. Original commentary, substantive editing and value for viewers matter; buying an AI subscription or adding a disclosure does not establish eligibility. Reviewers look at a channel's main theme, most viewed and newest videos, watch time and metadata, so judge your channel as a whole rather than one upload at a time.

## Mistakes That Waste Time and Money

- **Paying for generation before editing is solved.** Runway Standard buys under a minute of Gen-4.5 footage a month. Fix pacing and captions first; a few generated seconds rarely carry a video.
- **Publishing watermarked or unlicensed output.** OpusClip Free clips carry a watermark, and ElevenLabs Free narration does not include a commercial license.
- **Exporting small thumbnails.** One thumbnail below 720p downscales an entire A/B test to 854 x 480.
- **Skipping disclosure.** Realistic generated scenes need the AI use answer at upload.
- **Forgetting the clock on free exports.** OpusClip Free media expires after three days, so download the clips you want to keep.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best AI video creation tool for YouTube?

There is no single winner because the tools do different jobs. Runway generates short AI clips for background shots, CapCut and Descript edit recorded footage, OpusClip turns long videos into Shorts and Canva makes thumbnails. Most channels need one editor plus one specialist tool.

### Can I make YouTube videos with free AI tools?

Yes. Eligible YouTube Studio accounts have Ask Studio and title and thumbnail tests at no cost, CapCut has a free plan, Descript Free includes 60 media minutes a month and Canva Free covers thumbnails. Check the selected feature's credits, export limits, watermark and commercial rights; a free account does not unlock every AI feature.

### Do I have to disclose AI-generated video on YouTube?

Not every video made with AI needs disclosure. YouTube requires it for realistic generated or meaningfully altered content, such as a fabricated scene or a real person shown saying something they did not. Its policy also covers generated music when music is the main focus. Minor edits and content that is not realistic generally do not need disclosure under this rule.

### Can AI-generated videos be monetized on YouTube?

AI use alone does not disqualify a video. A channel must meet YouTube Partner Program eligibility, rights requirements and monetization policies. Original value matters; mass produced, generic or repetitive content can be ineligible as inauthentic content. Disclosing AI use does not by itself reduce a video's eligibility to earn.

### Which AI tool turns long YouTube videos into Shorts?

OpusClip is built for that job. Its free plan finds clips, adds captions and reframes them for vertical video, but exports carry a watermark and expire after three days. The Starter plan removes the watermark and adds a virality score.
