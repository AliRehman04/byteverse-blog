## 6. Final topic recommendation with a clear justification

**Excel CSV Special Characters Broken? Fix UTF-8 Safely**

This topic serves an immediate problem, reinforces the existing spreadsheet cluster and permits original factual examples without pretending to have used an unavailable product session. It adds the decision users need before saving: can the original bytes simply be re-read, or has the wrong text already been written back?

It is preferable here to a generic converter article because its job is diagnosis, not conversion. It is preferable to speculative form/ChatGPT troubleshooting because the byte mechanisms can be demonstrated reliably and bounded by Microsoft/Unicode documentation.

Limitations: no current Google ranking comparison, no GSC query evidence, strong existing competition, and no assurance that a new article alone resolves the site's low traffic. The narrower title and examples are editorial advantages, not ranking predictions.
